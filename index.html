Why Open Innovation & Gemma Matter for Outdoor Health

Closed AI APIs require continuous cell signal, upload your real-time coordinates to proprietary servers, and cost money per query. By building around Google's open-weight Gemma, AeroGemma can:

📶

Trail & Backcountry Ready

Gemma's open weights can be run offline on a laptop, Raspberry Pi, or phone without internet when you are deep in the woods or mountains.

🔒

Location & Health Privacy

Your GPS coordinates and personal respiratory sensitivities stay completely on your device, never tracked or monetized by third parties.

⚡

Zero API Subscription Costs

Open-weight models give developers, schools, and hikers perpetual access with zero paywalls, rate caps, or API deprecation fears.

⚙️

AeroGemma Settings

Stored securely in your local browser storage. Powers Gemma models directly.
